## Description (4,000)
Your coat has a passport. Tap it with your iPhone.

TAP THE TAG
Hold your iPhone near the tag sewn into your coat. Worn With Care reads it on your phone, checks it belongs to this coat, and opens its passport: "This is your coat."

CARE THAT REMEMBERS FOR YOU
Turn on care reminders and your iPhone gives you a quiet nudge at the right moments: brush it on Saturdays, steam it on the 1st of the month, store it for summer, bring it back for autumn. Scheduled on your phone; nothing leaves it.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN AND LOCK SCREEN
Add the Worn With Care widget to see your coat's next care step and how many times you've worn it, at a glance.

ITS WHOLE STORY
• The journey: 21,400 km from a wool farm in New Zealand, through a mill in Italy and an atelier in Portugal, to Paris, on a map you can explore.
• Who made it, and what it's made of.
• Care guidance that changes with how often you wear it.
• Its environmental footprint, with every estimate clearly marked.
• Where it goes next: resell, repair, thrift or recycle, and pass its story to the next owner.
• Your wardrobe: what to wear today, based on the weather.

A RECORD ANYONE CAN CHECK
The passport's record is signed and published on Base, a public blockchain, so anyone can check it hasn't changed.

IN THREE LANGUAGES
English, French and Portuguese, in light or dark.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts. No tracking. What you write stays on your phone.

## App Review notes
Worn With Care is a digital product passport for a coat, with features that only work on iPhone:

1. NFC tag reading (Core NFC): Menu → first icon. It reads the NDEF tag in the coat and confirms it belongs to this passport. Reviewers won't have the coat, so a 30-second video of a scan is attached. Any NTAG sticker written with https://wornwith.care/?dpp=4f2a will also work.
2. Care reminders (local notifications): Menu → bell → "Turn on reminders". Four repeating notifications are scheduled on the device.
3. Home-screen and lock-screen widget (WidgetKit): long-press the home screen → + → Worn With Care. It shows the next care step and wear count.

No account or sign-in is needed: the app opens straight onto the coat's passport. Environmental figures are labelled as estimates in the app.
